# ResumePilot AI

## AI-Powered Resume Analysis, Job Matching & Interview Preparation

ResumePilot AI is an AI-powered career assistance system designed to help students and job seekers analyze their resumes, discover suitable job roles, identify skill gaps, and prepare for interviews.

The platform combines resume analysis, job matching, interview guidance, practice modules, learning resources, and AI assistance in a single Streamlit-based application.

---

## Project Overview

Students often face difficulty understanding whether their resume is suitable for a particular job role, which skills they need to improve, and how to prepare for different stages of recruitment.

ResumePilot AI addresses these challenges by providing:

- Automated resume analysis
- Job-role recommendations
- Skill-gap identification
- Resume, ATS and Hireability scores
- Job Match Score
- Interview preparation
- AI-generated interview questions
- Learning resources
- AI career and interview assistance

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of ResumePilot AI are:

- Extract and analyze information from PDF/DOCX resumes.
- Identify relevant technical and job-related skills.
- Compare candidate skills with multiple job roles.
- Provide Resume, ATS, Hireability, and Job Match scores.
- Recommend suitable job roles.
- Identify missing or weak skills for a target role.
- Provide a general interview roadmap.
- Guide students through different stages of the recruitment process.
- Provide aptitude, technical, HR, and mock interview practice.

## Key Features

### 1. User Registration & Login

Students can create an account and securely log in to the application.

### 2. User Profile

The profile section allows students to maintain:

- Personal information
- Educational information
- Skills
- Experience
- Other career-related information

The stored profile information can be used for analysis and recommendations.

### 3. Resume Upload

Students can upload their resume in:

- PDF format
- DOCX format
- Txt format

### 4. Resume Analysis

The system extracts, cleans, and analyzes the uploaded resume.

It identifies relevant information and calculates:

- Resume Score
- ATS Score
- Hireability Score
- Job Match Score

### 5. Skill Extraction

The system identifies technical and job-related skills from the resume.

Examples include programming languages, databases, machine learning technologies, tools, and other relevant skills.

### 6. Skill-Gap Analysis

The system compares the student's existing skills with the skills required for the selected job role.

It displays:

- Skills already present
- Missing skills
- Weak skills

### 7. Job Matching

ResumePilot AI compares the resume with predefined job roles.

The system:

1. Compares candidate skills with job requirements.
2. Calculates match scores.
3. Ranks suitable job roles.
4. Displays the Top 3 Job Recommendations.

### 8. Resume-Based Questions

The system generates potential interview questions based on:

- Resume
- Skills
- Projects
- Experience

This helps students prepare for questions that may be asked about their own resume.

### 9. Interview Roadmap

The application provides a seven-stage interview roadmap:

1. Resume Screening
2. Online Aptitude Test
3. Technical Interview
4. Coding Round
5. HR Interview
6. Final Interview
7. Offer Letter

### 10. Aptitude Practice

Students can:

- Attempt aptitude tests
- View their scores
- Review their performance
- Receive improvement suggestions

### 11. Technical Round

Students can select a technology or topic and receive AI-generated technical questions with answers.

### 12. AI HR Mock Interview

Students can practice HR interviews using AI-generated questions.

The student answers questions through voice and receives AI-based feedback.

### 13. Interview Hub

The Interview Hub provides useful YouTube learning resources related to specific interview topics.

### 14. AI Assistant

The AI Assistant provides interactive assistance for:

- Interview-related questions
- Career-related queries
- General preparation guidance

### 15. Progress Tracking

The system helps students track their interview preparation activities and progress.
